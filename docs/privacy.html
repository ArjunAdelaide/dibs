Dibs privacy notice

Draft, 7 October 2026. Dibs is a test product run by one person.

What Dibs is

Dibs is an AI agent that you text. It finds things to do, helps you book them, and sends you alerts that you ask for.

What Dibs keeps

  • Your phone number or Apple ID email, because that is how Dibs replies to you.
  • The messages you send to Dibs and the replies.
  • A location, if you give one (a suburb, a city or a shared pin).
  • Your bookings and alerts.
  • Short notes about your preferences, for example your usual group size.

This is stored on the operator's computer. It is not sold and it is not used for advertising.

Who else sees your information

  • Google (Gemini): your messages go to an AI model to write the replies. On the free plan that Dibs uses now, Google may use this content to improve its products. Do not send Dibs anything sensitive.
  • Stripe: if you save a card, Stripe stores it. Dibs never sees your card number.
  • Venues: when you book, the venue gets the name, time, group size and a contact detail for the booking.
  • Map and event services (OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, Ticketmaster): Dibs sends them a place or coordinates to look up venues, a time zone and events. It does not send them your name or number.
  • The operator: a person may read a conversation to complete a booking or fix a problem.

Your choices

  • Text STOP and Dibs stops messaging you and cancels your alerts. Text START to come back.
  • Text remove my card to delete your saved card from Stripe.
  • To have your messages and notes deleted, email iamdibsagent@gmail.com from any address and include the number you texted from.

Payments

When you approve a booking, Dibs places a hold on your saved card for the exact total shown. You are charged only when the booking is confirmed. If it cannot be completed, the hold is released.
